## Research experience

**Undergraduate researcher, AIM Lab, Hanbat National University** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Advisor: Prof. Haneol Jang · Feb 2024 – present

- Designed and implemented SPOT-DETR, a detector for tiny objects in aerial images (co-first author). It reaches 37.6 AP on AI-TOD and 33.3 AP on AI-TOD-v2, above every detector retrained under the same protocol.
- Proposed YOLO-Dos, a speckle suppression module for SAR ship detection (first author). It raises mAP@50:95 by 5.75 points over YOLOv8 on HRSID.
- Proposed region weights for the eyes, nose and mouth in a ViT deepfake detector (first author). On FaceForensics++ it improves ACER by 7.94% and AUC by about 3% over plain ViT.
